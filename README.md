Content Writer V3 — The Evidence-First AI Content Platform

Stop publishing unsupported claims. V3 grounds every assertion in verified research and makes that evidence visible to your team. Every draft, every edit, every publish decision requires human approval—so you stay in control.

What makes V3 different:
- Claims are bounded by evidence (not pure LLM hallucination)
- Full audit trail: research → brief → draft → review → publish
- Editorial review flags brand voice fit, but humans decide approval
- Retries are human-triggered, never silent
- Built for content teams, not solo writers

The pipeline:
Research (deep agent-driven evidence collection) → Strategy (human decision on angle & audience) → Drafting (evidence-bounded generation) → Review (editorial flags, human approval) → Publishing (with outcomes tracking)

Long-term: designed to scale into a Jasper-class workflow platform where non-technical users can compose custom multi-agent pipelines.
